## Description

Bsky Dreams is built for people who want to think more, not less.

Most social apps are designed to keep you scrolling. Bsky Dreams is designed to help you understand — who's talking to whom, how ideas spread over time, and what's actually worth reading.

────────────────────────────

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Network Constellation
Map any topic as a living force graph. Watch clusters of connected thinkers emerge in real time. Tap any node to explore who they are and what they've been saying.

Timeline Scrubber
Lay posts along a true time axis. See how a conversation developed over minutes, hours, or days — not just what's newest.

Reader View
Every link, read without distraction. Readable mode strips ads and clutter. Archive mode reaches pages others can't. Three modes for any article on the web.

Saved Search Channels
Build curated streams around the topics, communities, and voices you care about. Your sidebar, your curation.

────────────────────────────

MORE FEATURES

• TV Mode — vertical video feed with topic selector and 2× speed on hold
• Gallery — pure image browsing with full-screen lightbox
• Smart home feed with Following and Discover tabs
• Advanced search with date range, author, and language filters
• Analytics — post performance charts and engagement heatmap
• Threaded conversations with depth-coded color nesting
• Direct Messages with real-time chat
• Full compose: images, video, GIFs, link previews, @mention autocomplete
• Share Extension — share anything from Photos or Safari directly into a post
• Cross-device sync via AT Protocol repository
• Background notifications

────────────────────────────

PRIVACY

No trackers. No ads. No third-party SDKs. Your credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain and transmitted only to bsky.social. Built on the open AT Protocol.
